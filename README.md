# 🧠 Analysis of the Kaggle 2021 Data Science Survey for the Arab Data Science Community

## 📌 Introduction
This report provides insights into the **data science and machine learning community in Arab countries** based on the **Kaggle 2021 Survey**.  
The analysis focuses on the distribution of **programming languages** used by Arab participants, highlighting their **demographic and professional characteristics**.  

Python and libraries such as **Pandas**, **Matplotlib**, and **Seaborn** were used for data analysis and visualization.

---

## 🧩 Methodology

### 📥 Data Collection
- The survey dataset contained **25,973 responses**.  
- The data was filtered to include only participants from **Arab countries** (e.g., Egypt, Saudi Arabia, UAE, etc.).

### 🧹 Data Cleaning
- The **first row (questions)** was stored separately for reference.  
- The first row was then **removed** from the dataframe to facilitate analysis.  
- **Missing values (NaN)** in the programming language columns were handled appropriately.

---

## 📊 Data Analysis
The analysis focused on **Question 7 (Q7)** — “Which programming languages do you use on a regular basis?”  
Responses were aggregated and visualized using **bar plots** created with **Matplotlib**.

### 🎨 Visualization
A bar plot illustrated the **distribution of programming languages** among Arab participants, with custom coloring for clarity and readability.

---

## 🔑 Key Insights

### 💻 Programming Language Distribution
- **Python is the most used language**, confirming its dominance in the data science field.  
- **SQL** and **R** ranked second and third — SQL for database management and R for statistical analysis.  
- **Other languages** such as JavaScript, Java, and C++ are used less frequently, reflecting the focus on analytical rather than application development tools.

### 👥 Demographic Characteristics
- **Age Group:** The majority of participants are **22–34 years old**, reflecting strong youth interest in data science.  
- **Gender:** Male participants dominate, but there is **notable female participation**, especially among students.  
- **Top Countries:** Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and UAE lead in participation, showing technological growth in the region.

### 🎓 Education and Experience
- Most participants hold a **Bachelor’s or Master’s degree**.  
- Programming experience typically ranges from **1–5 years**, indicating many are newcomers to the field.

### 💼 Professional Roles
Common roles include:
- **Software Engineer**
- **Data Scientist**
- **Data Analyst**
- **Students**, indicating strong academic interest in data science.
